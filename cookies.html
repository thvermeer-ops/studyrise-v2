Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. StudyRise also uses browser local storage, which works similarly but stores larger amounts of data locally on your device.

What we use

StudyRise uses only strictly necessary cookies and local storage. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or analytics cookies.

Name Type Purpose Duration
supabase-auth-token Cookie Keeps you logged in to your account Session / 1 week
tasks Local storage Stores your tasks locally for offline access Until you clear your data
grades Local storage Stores your grades locally Until you clear your data
subjects Local storage Stores your custom subjects Until you clear your data
userName Local storage Stores your display name Until you clear your data
streak Local storage Tracks your daily usage streak Until you clear your data
sr_user Local storage Stores your email for Pro verification Until you log out

Third-party cookies

Supabase (our database provider) may set its own cookies for authentication. GitHub Pages (our hosting provider) may collect basic access logs. Neither service uses these for advertising purposes.

Managing cookies

You can clear all local data at any time via Settings → Data → Clear all tasks, or by deleting your account. You can also clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings, though this will log you out of the app.
